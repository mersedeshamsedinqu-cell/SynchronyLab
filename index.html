The model

Each region holds 160 regular‑spiking pyramidal cells and 40 fast‑spiking interneurons (Izhikevich, 2003), integrated at 0.5 ms.

v' = 0.04v² + 5v + 140 − u + I u' = a(bv − u) if v ≥ 30 mV: v ← c, u ← u + d

Pooled synapses decay with τE = 2 ms and τI = 5 ms, which gives PING gamma near 35–40 Hz. An 8 Hz septal drive paces hippocampal theta. The HPC→PFC projection is 5.3× stronger than the return path.

What integrity changes

  • Recurrent E→E weights scale with integrity, standing in for synapse loss.
  • E→I drive keeps 35% at zero integrity, standing in for PV‑interneuron dysfunction.
  • Long‑range HPC↔PFC weights scale with integrity × coupling.

Analysis uses Welch spectra (512‑sample Hann, 50% overlap), magnitude‑squared coherence and the Tort modulation index (18 phase bins; θ 6–10 Hz, γ 30–80 Hz) on a rolling 4.1 s window.

Scope

This is a phenomenological teaching and hypothesis tool. It is not fitted to recordings, and its parameters are illustrative.

It explores one question from rodent work on Alzheimer's‑like pathology: behaviour can recover while fronto‑hippocampal synchrony stays impaired (Shamsedin, 2026, Neuroscience Research 231:105117, doi).
